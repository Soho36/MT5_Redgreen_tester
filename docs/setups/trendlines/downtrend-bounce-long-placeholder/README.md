# Downtrend (falling trendline) bounce, limit (long) (placeholder)

After price has moved above a falling trendline, it comes back down to the line and bounces; entered with a buy limit resting on the line.

**Not tested yet.** This folder holds the setup's place in the user's [setup sketch](../../../reference/setup_map_sketch.jpg) until a study is proposed; a study gets its own `qNN-name/` folder with `PROTOCOL.md` and `RESULTS.md`. [All setups](../../../README.md).

Related evidence so far: no short at levels or lines has made money on NQ (Q21, Q23), and resting limit orders at a level fill mostly when price keeps going through it (Q20).
