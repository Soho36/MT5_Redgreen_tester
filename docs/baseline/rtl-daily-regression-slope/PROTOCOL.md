Maintain one direction for all M30 submissions on the same tester trading date. Roll across weeks, weekends and holidays with no reset. Require strictly positive slope for a new buy stop. Block on zero, falling slope or insufficient history. Trade RTL on M30 with normal pending-order replacement, calendar, flatten and exits.

Replay gate off and on with the previous M30 regression study's configuration. Use one MNQ-equivalent contract ($2 per point), $1.05 cost per completed trade, continuous history and native M1 OHLC modeling. Compare net and maximum after-cost closed-trade drawdown from zero for June 2010–2015, 2016–2019, and 2020–September 2026, plus pooled results. Attribute by order submission.
