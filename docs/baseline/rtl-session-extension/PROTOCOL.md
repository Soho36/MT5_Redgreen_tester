At order submission measure the bid open relative to the first open of the synthetic exchange session. A rise of at least 2% is primary, with 1% and 3% neighbors. Define green P&L as strictly positive realized session dollars. Also examine at least +1 cumulative net R and at least two consecutive closed winners. Use completed trades taken by the rule itself. R uses each trade's initial risk.

Compare all four extension × state cells for baseline next-trade win rate, mean dollars and mean R. Within extended trades compare state on/off with 95% calendar-week bootstrap intervals on mean R. Compare within common submission hours as a sensitivity to time of day. Report both periods and all thresholds. Intervals are exploratory across these comparisons.
