# GG full-range outside-bar screen

2026-10-20. Exploratory MT5 screen of the user's green outside-bar examples. The trading rule is the existing no-cap GG long strategy; only signal eligibility changes. No parameter or filter is adopted.

## Definition fixed before the runs

The last **closed green M30 candle** is the GG signal. For lookback (n), compare its full high and low with the preceding (n) closed M30 candles. It qualifies only if its high is strictly above **every** prior high and its low is strictly below **every** prior low. The candle body and preceding candle colours are irrelevant. The current forming candle is excluded.

To quantify “much larger,” let (W) be the widest high-to-low range of any one of those (n) preceding candles. Each high and low overhang must be at least (oW), and the signal's full range must be at least (rW). The grid is (n=1,3,5,10) with ((r,o)=(1,0),(1.25,0),(1.25,0.10)). An (o=0) case still requires strictly positive overhang on both sides. These thresholds are an initial grid, not measurements inferred from the screenshots.

The EA otherwise keeps GG's buy stop at the signal high, stop at the signal low, RR 1 bar-close exit, session cutoff and early-close handling. A rejected green signal cancels an older pending buy stop, as other GG filter rejections do. Data and settings come from the saved [no-cap GG run](../signal-colour/GREEN_RUN_FOLLOWUP.md): MNQ-priced continuous NQ, M30, one-minute OHLC MT5 model, one contract, 2016-01-01 through the available data ending 2026-10-01. Net results subtract $1.05 per completed trade. The research EA is generated by [prepare_gg_outside.py](../../../python/prepare_gg_outside.py), and [analyze_gg_outside.py](../../../python/analyze_gg_outside.py) reconciles the ledgers. The 13 saved runs, INIs, reports and output hashes are in `Reports/gg_outside_20261020/`.

## Results

The strict (n=1) outside bar earns money in both periods, but in 2020–26 its PF is 1.058 versus GG's 1.083, with far fewer trades. Making that bar larger does not improve recent PF. Every (n=3) case earns in 2016–19 but loses in 2020–26. Most (n=5) and (n=10) cases also lose recently. The one positive recent (n=10) cell has just 75 trades and negative mean net R (−0.033); it is not a reliable exception. The (n=3), ratio-1.00 case loses in five of the seven years 2020–26. Drawdown reductions generally accompany very large reductions in trade count and profit.

## Interpretation and limits

On these data, the full-range green outside-bar filter does **not** improve the existing GG strategy. The visual idea may still concern a large *subsequent move in either direction*; this screen tests the specific GG buy-stop trade, not that separate volatility hypothesis. A wide signal also creates a wide GG stop, so a large move alone need not yield a profitable long trade.

This is an exploratory parameter grid on already examined history. The tester uses one-minute OHLC paths, not exchange ticks; earlier project work found material execution-mode sensitivity. The sample has no untouched historical holdout, and no result here establishes live profitability. Keep the GG baseline unchanged. A separate follow-up, if desired, would measure post-close upward and downward excursions against other equally wide green candles before proposing a different trade rule.
