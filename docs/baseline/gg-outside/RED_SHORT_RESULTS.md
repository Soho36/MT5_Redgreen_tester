# Red outside bar: sell-stop test

2026-10-21. This is the strict new-high version of the red-candle short hypothesis. The marked example is covered by the [breakdown comparison](RED_BREAKDOWN_RESULTS.md), where a new high is optional. The [earlier short screen](SHORT_RESULTS.md) sold under a **green** outside candle; that is a separate setup. Here the signal candle is **red**, as in the user's example.

## Fixed rule and comparison

The last closed M30 candle must have close below open. Its full high must exceed every high of the preceding n closed candles, and its full low must be below every preceding low. As in the [outside-bar definition](RESULTS.md), W is the widest individual candle in that prior window. The signal range must be at least r × W, and each overhang must be at least o × W. The 12 settings use n = 1, 3, 5, 10 and (r, o) = (1.00, 0), (1.25, 0), (1.25, 0.10). Positive overhang on both sides is required even when o = 0.

A sell stop rests at the **red signal low**, with stop loss at its high. The first M30 close at or below entry − 1R triggers the market exit. All other order lifetime, run-cap-off, time-window, flattening and early-close rules are the same as in the green-candle short screen. The unfiltered control sells under every qualifying red candle with the same rules. Data: MNQ-priced continuous NQ, M30 with one-minute OHLC MT5 execution, one contract, 2016-01-01 through available data ending 2026-10-01. Results are net of $1.05 per completed trade.

The isolated EA and 13 saved MT5 runs are generated by [prepare_gg_red_outside_short.py](../../../python/prepare_gg_red_outside_short.py), and [analyze_gg_red_outside_short.py](../../../python/analyze_gg_red_outside_short.py) reconciles their ledgers. INIs, EA, reports, ledgers, logs and output hashes are in `Reports/gg_red_outside_short_20261021/`. For manual testing, select `CodexBaselineResearch\GG_red_outside_short`; `UseOutsideFilter` defaults to **true**, with n=3, range ratio=1.25 and overhang fraction=0.

In contrast, the previous **green** outside-bar n=3 short had recent net +$2,530 on 190 trades, but that was a different candle colour and failed the earlier period. The corrected **red** n=3 short has recent net −$10,252 on 540 trades.

## Verification and decision

All 13 red-short ledgers reconcile trade counts and gross profit with MT5 totals; the EA compiled with zero errors and warnings. The generated source explicitly requires `close < open`, counts consecutive red candles, and compares the signal high/low with only preceding closed bars. MT5 HTML order records show 24,725 control sell stops and 1,168 strict-n=3 sell stops, all with stop losses above entry and no buy-stop orders in either report.

Keep this red-outside short rule off. No ratio/lookback in the tested grid provides positive historical net results. The data have been examined in prior project studies, so these comparisons are exploratory; the one-minute OHLC fill model and lack of untouched historical data remain the same limitations as for the long and green-short screens.
